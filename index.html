Spacing

Spacing should stay consistent and generous enough to create clarity without overwhelming the content.

Compact

Use for tight UI blocks and small supporting text.

Balanced

Use for card layouts, content sections, and grouped info.

Generous

Use for hero areas, major launches, and key messaging.

Email signature

Keep the signature minimal, stacked, and easy to scan, with the confidentiality notice grouped at the bottom.
