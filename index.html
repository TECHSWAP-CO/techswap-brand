TechSwap Brand Design System

The definitive brand standards and visual guidelines for TechSwap. Engineered for consistency, innovation, and trust across all digital touchpoints.

v2.0 Official Primary Color: #04007f Google Font: Kanit

Brand Identity & Core Values

Our fundamental pillars and voice principles

Technology Exchange

Facilitating seamless technological transformations and hardware upgrades with reliability and intelligence.

Seamless Speed

Represented by our pixelated speed-curve 'S' mark, emphasizing agile digital transitions.

Security & Trust

Deep dark navy anchor tones reflecting enterprise-grade data protection and privacy integrity.

Modern Simplicity

Clean geometric visuals, balanced typography, and purposeful layouts across all collateral.

Logo System & Usage

Official primary logo and mark applications

Primary Full Logo (LOGO-PNG-02)

Preferred mark for websites, header navigation, presentations, and formal communications on light backgrounds.

Dark Surface Application

Monochrome or high-contrast white variant for dark backgrounds, dark mode UI, and dark hero banners.

Brand Mark Symbol (LOGO-PNG-01)

Standalone 'S' mark for favicons, app icons, social avatars, and compact UI headers.

Clear Space Rule

Maintain a minimum clearance equal to 50% of the symbol's height around all sides of the mark.

Web Min: 120px wide

Minimum Dimensions

Never render the full wordmark smaller than 120px wide on digital screens to preserve legibility.

Logo Usage Guidelines

✓ Approved Usage

Always maintain original aspect ratio, use official brand palette colors, and ensure sufficient contrast against background surfaces.

✕ Prohibited Modifications

Do NOT stretch or distort the logo, change font styles, re-color the pixel gradient elements, or place on busy image backgrounds.
